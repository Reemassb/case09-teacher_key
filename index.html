EVIDENCE & EQUATIONS  ·  CASE #09

The Stopped Clock — Teacher Answer Key

Paste your team names, one per line, exactly as students typed them. Spacing, case and punctuation are ignored. The verdict turns on the corrected time of impact against the access interval — never the dial reading, which sits inside that interval for about half of all teams whichever way the verdict falls.

Set this to the paper you issued. The two papers generate different evidence from the same team name, so a key made on the wrong setting produces plausible numbers that match nothing. Check the case file reference: it ends -S on the standard paper and -E on the extended one, and the same code appears on the student’s screen and at the top of their exported report. If the letters differ, the paper is wrong, not the student.
